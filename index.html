    Visual refresher · control chart intuition
    x̄ control chart · shaft diameter, subgroups of 4in control
    target 10.000 mmσ 0.020n 4σ/√n 0.010limits 10.000 ± 0.030
    Each dot is the mean of four shafts. The limits sit at ±3 standard errors, so a point outside is a rare event for a stable process — about 3 in 1000. Watch how a 1σ shift hides for several points before a signal, while a 2σ shift is caught almost at once. That gap between shift and detection is the whole reason the later chapters exist.
